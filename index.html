Thessaloniki Greece

ULP Year 3 · Living in the City 26–27

A working map of Thessaloniki for the Social Intervention Project. The city was European Capital of Culture in 1997 and spent the following three decades arguing about what that left behind. We are mapping what is still standing, what is still running, and what quietly closed.

The city

Location, size, and a short historical sketch — founded 315 BC, Roman, Byzantine, Ottoman until 1912, burnt in 1917, remade by the 1922 population exchange. Then say what kind of city Thessaloniki is now, and give the evidence rather than asserting it.

Say which population figure you are using and why. The municipality, the metropolitan area and the regional unit are three different numbers and most reports quietly mix them.

What the city is dealing with

The challenges behind the cultural and placemaking programmes: a long-argued "arrested development" relative to Athens, the working port at the city's edge, transport that only got fixed in 2024, short-let pressure on the centre. Two or three of these, with the structural cause rather than the symptom.
