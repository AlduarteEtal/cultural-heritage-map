    The city

    Location, size, and a short historical sketch. Then say what kind of city this is — historic, post-industrial, cultural, creative — and give the evidence for that description rather than asserting it.

    Keep this to three or four paragraphs. The map carries the detail.

    What the city is dealing with

    The challenges behind the cultural or placemaking programme: housing, accessibility, employment, image, competition with other cities. Two or three of them, with the structural cause rather than the symptom.

    Where the desk and the street disagree

    The most useful paragraph on this page. Name one thing the statistics or the city's own communications claim, and what you found when you went to look. This is also the honest answer to the marking criterion about mixing desk and field research.

    How we worked

    Methods, in plain terms: which datasets, how many walks, how many conversations, what you deliberately did not cover. Say who did what.

    What we would do differently

    Short and specific. Formative work is judged partly on knowing its own limits.
